# Logic Sugar v2.1.7

## 中文

- 修复误触语句本体时语句立即移动到顶部的问题。
- 普通单语句拖动需要离开语句宽度的 40% 才会开始；轻微移动后松手会保持原位置。
- 已选语句和批量拖动同样获得误触保护，中键复制行为保持不变。

## English

- Fixed accidental taps on a statement moving it to the top of the logic editor.
- A single-statement drag now starts only after moving 40% of the statement width; slight movement keeps the original order.
- Selected and multi-statement drags receive the same accidental-input guard, while middle-click copying remains unchanged.

The universal JAR works on desktop and Android.
